# Plague Marines - Advice

## TL;DR

- **Play cagey in TP1–2, then break out in TP3.** Survive, spread poison, and let the enemy come to you. With a 5" move, if you push early, more numerous teams out-activate you and catch you out of position. [strong consensus]
- **Always Be Poisoning.** Get as many Poison tokens out as you can before TP3. Contagion, Toxic, the Champion's heal and Curse of Rot range all scale with poison. A poisoned enemy on 1–2 wounds dies on its own activation and wastes it. [strong consensus]
- **Toxic needs the token at the START of the action.** A token you apply during an attack does not give +1 Dmg in that action. Use Virulent Poison or Poisonous Miasma first. The Champion's Charge → Virulent Poison → Fight is the signature kill. [strong consensus]
- **Contagion and Lumbering Death are your core CP spends.** Most other ploys are situational. Keep 1 CP for Curse of Rot or Sickening Resilience when you can. [strong consensus]
- **Plague Bells and Plague Rounds go in every list.** [strong consensus]
- **Champion, Malignant Plaguecaster, Icon Bearer and Bombardier are near-fixed. The flex slots are Fighter, Heavy Gunner and Warrior, by matchup.** [strong consensus]
- **Plant Banner and Dominate are the only real TacOps.** Plant Banner when you can plant it TP2 and hold it. Dominate into teams that out-activate you. Never take Sweep & Clear, Martyrs or Envoy. [strong consensus]
- **The Icon Bearer makes first contact.** Put him on the contested objective under Cloud of Flies or smoke with Contagion up. Keep counter-punch pieces within reach of him. [strong consensus]

## Operative Selection

- **Icon Bearer — always take.** He counts as APL 4 to contest objectives and always gives Contagion without poison. He is first onto the midpoint, with a 5-attack Severe melee profile and a Bolt pistol. Put him on the point under Cloud of Flies or smoke with Contagion. The enemy must then take an obscured shot or come within 3" and hit 1 worse. Do not make him your only threat: put a second threat near the middle so the opponent must choose. Do not push him far forward alone only for free Contagion — that is a trap. Contagion costs 0CP only while he is in your opponent's territory. Paying for it in TP2 and moving him into position for free Contagion in TP3–4 is often safer. [strong consensus]
- **Malignant Plaguecaster — always take (except into Celestian Insidiants).** He is your "melta": two psychic shots through Astartes, then Poisonous Miasma for 3 mortal wounds. With Saturate and Ceaseless he can take a model from full to dead. Poisonous Miasma targets an enemy that is visible within 7", OR any valid target at any range. That includes an enemy on Engage across the board, or an enemy in a friendly operative's control range. It does not work on an enemy in his own control range. He can use it from Conceal, on an activation or a counteract. He can finish elites on 3 wounds or less "for free". Putrescent Vitality heals once per turning point; he can heal himself. Keep him between home and mid, in heal range — never on the front line, never as bait, and never forward in TP2 against long chargers (Wolf Scouts). [strong consensus]
- **Champion — always take.** Grandfather's Blessing: when a poisoned enemy within 7" loses wounds, he regains the same number, up to 3 per turning point. He does not need to cause the damage. In a fight, the first hit poisons and the later hits heal him. Charge → Virulent Poison → Fight takes the sword from 4/5 to 5/6 from the first die, and it two-taps 12-wound models. Pair him with the Malignant Plaguecaster (damage plus healing). Place him to counter-charge whoever kills the Icon Bearer, not stacked with him. The plasma pistol's Hot damage comes after the attack, so he cannot heal it in the same action. [strong consensus]
- **Bombardier — near-fixed.** Unlimited built-in Krak and Blight Grenades, hitting on 3+. Blights have Toxic, Severe, Saturate and Blast, and they are Lethal 5+ on close boards. Kraks on 3s into a Bolt pistol with Ceaseless are a strong anti-elite line. Two-step attack: shoot a Plague Rounds bolter to poison the target first, then throw the Blight — it gets Toxic only if the target already has a token. He holds home well, then switches to offence. He breaches for 1 AP (Grenadier). [strong consensus]
- **Fighter — top flex pick into elites, melee teams and clumps.** The flail counts as a Fight action for action limits and Astartes, so: Charge → flail → flail. Each flail hits every visible enemy within 2" with no save and no retaliation. That is about 8 damage per target on average (min 6, max 10), and it brings most models into a 2-hit kill. Then sit in combat (safe from shooting) with Lumbering Death and Contagion. He can flail on a counteract too, and from outside engagement ("flail and bail"). Charge in at the end of TP2, take a counteract, then do it again in TP3. Aim for 2+ enemies in range. He also hits friendlies within 2" (but cannot poison them). He has a Bolt pistol, and Krak or Blight equipment gives him a charge-fight-throw double-kill line. Drop him into shooting hordes and teams that stay away. [strong consensus]
- **Heavy Gunner — flex pick into elites with good saves, and on close boards.** The plague spewer is 5 attacks on 2+, 3/3, Severe, Torrent, and it poisons several targets without buffs. Through Astartes he shoots the spewer and then his Bolt pistol. Move-dash-shoot gives about a 10" threat zone. Paired with Curse of Rot, his 5 dice against 3 defence dice act as "pseudo-pierce": every rolled 3 becomes a failed save plus damage. That is the line against Aegis, 2+ saves and 3+ save marines. He is best on Tomb World / Gallowdark, into Deathwatch, Raveners (Saturate beats Chromatospore) and Stealth Suits (Torrent beats Savior Protocols). He is a counter-puncher and flanker, not a point-holder — his fists retaliate poorly. Leave him out vs fast melee teams (Murderwing) and on long-lane Volkus maps. [warming consensus — the Heavy Gunner vs Warrior choice is heavily debated]
- **Warrior — flex pick into hordes, shooting hordes, 4+ saves and long firing lanes.** Infinite-range Toxic boltgun (4 attacks, 3/4, 4/5 vs poisoned targets) plus a 5+ critical save against shooting. He double-shoots well with Ceaseless (Lumbering Death) and Severe, and two shots usually kill one horde model each. His Severe, poison plague knife lets him take a charge and hold a point better than the Heavy Gunner. Good into Blades of Khaine, Brood Brothers, Inquisitorial Agents, Hand of the Archon and Aquilons. Weak into 3+ save elites (8 dice vs 6 defence dice) — drop him there for the Heavy Gunner. [strong consensus]
- **Choosing the flex slots.** You drop two of Fighter, Heavy Gunner and Warrior, never the others. Elites/melee → Fighter + Heavy Gunner. Shooting hordes / long lanes → Warrior + one of the others. Melee strength order: Fighter, Champion, Malignant Plaguecaster, Icon Bearer, Bombardier, Heavy Gunner, Warrior.

## TacOp Selection

- **Dominate — the default into teams that out-activate you, and a strong pick into elites.** Killing an enemy with 12+ wounds gives 2 tokens, so elite kills pay double — but your own marines give the enemy 2 tokens each too. Play passively in TP2: take little damage, spread poison and set up. Start scoring from TP3; if you score early you over-extend and lose operatives. You need only about 3 kills. Kill and move away, and keep the Malignant Plaguecaster near for heals. Poison kills credit the operative that applied the poison — track who poisoned whom. It is a low-brain-load pick, because resilience and healing make it workable into anyone. [strong consensus]
- **Plant Banner — take it when you can plant on TP2 and survive.** It forces the enemy to come to you, so you do not need kills. It is best into teams with equal or fewer operatives and into passive, keep-away teams (Stealth Suits, Canoptek). Plant in a corner or room where your bodies or Razor Wire keep them out of contest range. Plant on a counteract or last activation of TP2. Then keep the Icon Bearer on it with Contagion, Lumbering Death and Sickening Resilience. Go clearly past the centre line so Poach and last-activation charges cannot undercut you. You can plant on vantage or stronghold upper floors if it is more than 5" from the killzone edge. You can pick it up and re-plant it deeper later. Do not plant into teams that out-activate you, or into the Murderwing Shrieker. A TP3 banner is a desperation play. Even when you take Dominate, deploy to threaten a banner — opponents over-commit to stop it. [strong consensus]
- **Sweep & Clear — avoid.** You must clear and hold the centre or enemy objective uncontested turn after turn. With a 5" move it loses every low-interaction game. [strong consensus]
- **Envoy — avoid.** Too slow with a 5" move; it feeds marines into a grinder. [strong consensus]
- **Martyrs / Rout — never.**
- Pick the TacOp together with the Crit Op: plan how each player scores it and how the opponent breaks your plan. Orb, Transmission and (partly) Reboot are bad Crit Ops for an elite team. Take TacOp as your primary, not Kill or Crit.

## Equipment Selection

- **Plague Bells — always take.** [strong consensus]
- **Plague Rounds — always take**, even without the Warrior: the Bombardier's and pistols' shots spread poison and Severe. [strong consensus]
- **Krak Grenades — default into elites.** They give the Fighter a ranged option, and with Lumbering Death's Ceaseless, dash plus Krak is a good counterpunch. Kraks are swingy, so fire them with Ceaseless. [strong consensus]
- **Blight Grenades — into hordes and high operative counts, especially on close boards.** They are "a better frag" (poison plus Severe) and give the Fighter a ranged option. The Bombardier already has them, so they are rarely needed otherwise. Their Toxic works only against targets that already have a token.
- **Poison Vents — strong into melee teams, hug teams and teams that stay close.** An enemy without a token that activates within 3" rolls a D3 and gains a token on a 3; an enemy with a token takes D3 damage. This punishes tar-pitting and charge-hugging, and gives the fist-only operatives a way to poison in melee. Take it into Goremongers, Exaction Squad, Celestian Insidiants, Brood Brothers and Wolf Scouts. Skip it into ranged elites. [warming consensus]
- **Staging terrain (light/heavy barricades, Razor Wire, ladders, Portable Barricade) — the usual 4th slot.** Staging comes before smokes: smokes are useless if you cannot reach the spot. Light barricades choke pinch points and stop charges. Razor Wire blocks access to your home objective or protects the banner. A Portable or heavy barricade on the Icon Bearer gives him a 2+ save in cover, which is good vs anti-obscure teams. Place barricades so you keep exit paths.
- **Smoke Grenades — into heavy shooting and melta teams that cannot ignore obscuring.** Smoke obscures outside 2" and cuts melta Piercing, which Cloud of Flies does not. Smoke under the Icon Bearer with Contagion baits the melta into a bad shot. Skip it into Kasrkin and other strong anti-obscure teams.
- **Stun Grenades — situational**, into APL-starved teams (Hierotek), Deathwatch, marine teams and hordes. Stun plus Nurglings stops an enemy from falling back. Not into Wolf Scouts.
- **Breaching charge** — only on close boards with more breaches than the Bombardier can handle.
- **Ammo cache** — helps an aggressive Champion or Warrior, but shows your plan.

## Faction Rule Advice

- **Toxic needs the token at the start of the action.** Weapon stats do not change during an action, so a token gained in the same shoot or fight gives no +1 Dmg. This includes Blight Grenades. To get Toxic on the first attack, poison the target first with Virulent Poison, Poisonous Miasma or an earlier Plague Rounds shot. Poison itself and the Champion's heal DO apply mid-action. [strong consensus]
- **Poison every enemy you can, and leave poisoned enemies on 1–2 wounds** instead of killing them. They die on their own activation, which wastes it and gives you counteracts. Poison does damage only when the enemy activates — not on a counteract or Guard. An enemy holds only one token; friendly Plague Marines cannot get your tokens. [strong consensus]
- **Toxic with Blast checks each target separately.** A poisoned secondary target takes Toxic damage even if the primary is clean.
- **Contagion effects only apply if the enemy STARTS the action in range.** An enemy that starts its move outside 3" gets its full move. Contagion counts as pseudo-injury, so it bypasses injury immunity (Angels of Death, the Wolf Scouts Fangbearer).
- **Astartes:** the Heavy Gunner can shoot the spewer, then the Bolt pistol. A grenade plus a bolt weapon also works. The Malignant Plaguecaster shoots two different psychic weapons, then uses Poisonous Miasma. Only PSYCHIC ranged weapons are limited to once per activation.
- **Disgustingly Resilient** rolls per damage die. It does not reduce poison or mortal-wound damage.
- **The team is CP-starved.** Defer initiative for CP unless you must go first. You may spend no CP in TP2 and bank it for TP3.

## Ploys

- **Command Re-roll** — do not spend CP on attack re-rolls; Lumbering Death covers that.

### Strategic Ploys

- **Contagion — your core ploy.** Use it from TP2 on. Enemies within 3" that have a Poison token and are visible, or are near the Icon Bearer, get −2" move and hit 1 worse. In practice it is mostly an Icon Bearer tool, because broad poison coverage is hard. Take it TP2 when the Icon Bearer contests mid. If you go last on TP2, walk him forward and plant with no CP; if you go first, pay for Contagion to keep him alive. [strong consensus]
- **Lumbering Death — the other core ploy; once fighting starts, always buy it.** It gives Ceaseless (and always Ceaseless while retaliating) if you moved 3" or less. Most of its value comes from retaliations, Guard shots and the Warrior's double shot. It works on Guard but not on a counteract. Climbs and hatchway costs count toward the 3"; a teleport does not. Use it only when you can cash it in at once. Take it TP2 only vs melee teams that rush; otherwise from TP3. [strong consensus]
- **Cloud of Flies.** Place it in the Strategy phase with no AP. Operatives wholly within 1" of the marker are obscured to shots from over 3" away. Only your marines benefit, and it does not obscure your own outgoing shots. Best uses: on the Icon Bearer with Contagion up (enemies take an obscured shot or come into Contagion range); on a gun platform (Warrior on Engage); where you plan to charge; or reactively to save an operative caught out after you lose initiative. Best vs teams without anti-obscure (Aquilons, Hand of the Archon, Celestian Insidiants, Canoptek, Stealth Suits, Mandrakes). Wasted vs Kasrkin, Inquisitorial Agents, Battleclade and Scout Squad; use smoke there to cut Piercing. [strong consensus]
- **Nurglings — do not sleep on it.** It limits enemy choices: a 2-APL operative cannot fall back, a 3-APL one loses its follow-up shot. Use it after a Champion charge-hug or Fighter engagement, to stop single models scoring objectives, and to force Deathwatch to choose between cleansing poison and the APL change. Useless vs Exaction Squad.

### Firefight Ploys

- **Virulent Poison — the enabler.** An enemy within 3", or visible and within 7", gains a Poison token automatically. It ignores "valid target" rules. Charge → Virulent Poison → Fight gives Toxic from the first die (the Champion's sword goes to 5/6). Use it in TP1–2 to poison without over-extending, e.g. reposition the Warrior within 7" on a counteract, use it, and shoot with Toxic next TP. Poison envoy carriers with it every turn. Spend it only when Toxic changes the breakpoint. [strong consensus]
- **Sickening Resilience — keep CP for it.** Disgustingly Resilient succeeds automatically and all attack damage drops by 1 for that whole enemy activation (double shoot or fight included). Not vs mortal wounds or Devastating. With Contagion it keeps the Icon Bearer alive on the banner and through a melta; with heals between activations, most enemies need 3 activations to kill a marine. [strong consensus]
- **Curse of Rot — situational, but swingy.** Use it after the enemy rolls, before re-rolls: their 3s become fails that cannot be re-rolled. It works on their attack and defence dice, but only when YOU fight or shoot (within 3", or 7" if the target is poisoned) — never on retaliation or when you are shot. Best into 3+ saves (marines) and 3+ hitters, and on big dice pools: the Heavy Gunner spewer plus Curse of Rot can turn 6 damage into 10. Ask the opponent to hold re-rolls until you count the 3s. Prefer it over Virulent Poison when fighting an unpoisoned target. Do not hold CP for it over Contagion. [strong consensus]
- **Poisonous Demise — weak.** 1 damage to each nearby enemy, used before your operative is removed. Useful after a Fighter flail on several models, or vs the Wolf Scouts leader if you cannot poison early.

## Terrain

### Volkus / Octarius (open)

- Take ladders: a 5" move cannot climb big ruins or reach vantage without them.
- The Warrior is better on open maps with long firing lanes; climb a vantage by ladder TP1 to cover mid. Drop him for the Heavy Gunner if there are no long lanes.
- Vantage is a trap for the Malignant Plaguecaster: climbing costs inches and takes him out of heal range.
- Contagion and the flail do not work through Volkus doors — control range there does not give visibility.
- You can plant the banner on a vantage or stronghold upper floor if it is more than 5" from the killzone edge. Whether you can plant inside terrain pieces C/D depends on the TO — check before the game.
- A Volkus stronghold corner banner limits Murderwing's movement advantage. Murderwing, Vespid Stingwings and Wrecka Krew are all stronger on open boards; Vespid dominate on Octarius.
- Heavy cover at mid blocks the Mandrakes' Dirgemaw stun. You can ignore the Angels of Death Eliminator by playing out of his lines behind heavy terrain.
- Retaliating first from a stronghold avoids Striking Scorpion fight-action damage.

### Tomb World / Gallowdark (close quarters)

- This is where Plague Marines shine. Deathball one side and choke the enemy; teleporters allow fast flank changes.
- The Heavy Gunner is at his best here (Torrent and Lethal at close range); put him further forward and on Guard on a point.
- Blight Grenades are Lethal 5+ here — take them as equipment into mid-range teams and hordes.
- The Bombardier breaches walls for 1 AP; never drop him here. Breach next to an objective, then Guard behind the breach. Take a breaching charge only on maps with many breaches.
- Light barricades choke corridors; a heavy barricade shields the Malignant Plaguecaster from seek light.
- Put the Fighter on the edge of your teleporter: an enemy that teleports in lands in his control range.
- On TW2, the banner works only vs teams without seek light; hide behind the pillar to plant. On TW1 vs Stealth Suits, plant in the hallway between the deployment zones.
- Poison Vents measure through open doors, not walls.
- Murderwing and Vespid Stingwings are much more manageable here; the Angels of Death Eliminator is scarier (seek light). Aquilon drops can move through walls, but the operative cannot set up across one.

## Generic Advice

- **Play cagey TP1–2, break out TP3** — but you still must reach the midfield, or short moves and Lumbering Death do nothing. Play to maximise your points and starve the opponent; play to win, not "not to lose". [strong consensus]
- **Play around two objectives (home and mid) and ignore the enemy home.** Default split is 4-2: Icon Bearer, Champion and Malignant Plaguecaster near mid with counter-punchers, a minimum force (Bombardier and/or Warrior) at home. 2-2-2 is too thin vs elites. Vs elites, stack hard (5-1, 4-2) and overwhelm one flank; vs hordes, spread more (1-3-2). [strong consensus]
- **Every move commits.** With 5" movement, position with intent, keep several options and stage carefully.
- **Counter-punch, do not open.** Fighter, Champion, Warrior and Icon Bearer go forward; Heavy Gunner, Malignant Plaguecaster and Bombardier counter-punch. Cover every operative the enemy can charge with Contagion and a friendly threat. Do not take shots that force you to over-commit — make the enemy expose pieces to kill a marine, then punish with Ceaseless.
- **Vs meltas, expect to lose a piece.** Always have a follow-up that kills the melta before it gets a second kill. Place the Icon Bearer so the melta must land in the open.
- **Deploy predictable pieces first** (Champion, Icon Bearer), then Warrior and Bombardier, then Fighter and Heavy Gunner last.
- **Plan counteracts as carefully as activations.** Priorities: plant the banner second-to-last, mission actions, then the Malignant Plaguecaster's heal. Count your counteracts ahead of time.
- **Parry out the last dice in a fight** to stay in combat, where ranged attackers cannot shoot you.
- Spread out vs blast weapons, and hide fully behind walls instead of giving door shots.
