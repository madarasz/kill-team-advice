# Deathwatch - Advice

## TL;DR

1. **Deathwatch is a SHOOTING team, not melee.** Every full-melee-skew report lost. Melee is a *defensive finishing tool* — charge to "hug" (hide in melee), then kill on your next activation/counteract. You can't heal while hugging. **[strong consensus]**
2. **The Disruptor is the best operative and near-auto-take (~9/10 games).** Omni-scramble is "the strongest ability in the game" and the reason DW beats elites. Only real drop: Raveners (they writhe out of sight). **[strong consensus]**
3. **Cagey TP1–2, explode TP3–4.** Project threat, bait, commit hard only on an opening. Judge plays by expected value, not the dice result. **[strong consensus]**
4. **You lack rerolls → win with the fewest, highest-quality dice.** Push extra dice (Horde-Slayer's 5th, Gunner's 5-attack plasma); spend CP mostly on Command Reroll. DW fades in long events ("eventually gets diced"). **[strong consensus]**
5. **DW is a 2-box toolbox team.** Its power is tailoring a different 5 to each matchup/map/primary; losing is usually a selection/deployment "skill issue," not a bad matchup. **[strong consensus]**

## Operative Selection

**Auto / near-auto includes**
- **Disruptor** — best op on the team. Omni-scramble = D6 activations locked out of *acting* (not just activating). Works on line-of-sight OR within 6" (not both). Lean over doorways on ITD/Tomb World to shut down whole rooms; neuter a melta marine to a 7" threat. Not a damage piece — it *frees your Gunner* to kill. Its Lethal-5 carbine + PC1 SIA still kills mooks. Also grants free Advanced Auspex Scan. **Skip only vs Raveners.** **[strong consensus]**
- **Horde-Slayer** — the default Gravis. Carries Infernus heavy bolter **and** a bolt pistol → move-shoot-shoot with no APL penalty; heals d3/round; 18 wounds. 5 dice = consistency for a reroll-starved team. Best ≤12-wound enemies and vs teams that ignore piercing / have "just a scratch" / improved cover. SIA: Lethal-5 on the bolter; Dev1 or weight-of-dice on the flamer to finish 3/6-HP models. **[strong consensus]**
- **Blademaster (Blademaster)** — top-3 melee model in the game, but the real trick is the **P1 double-shooting bolt pistol**. Parry-first + Brutal → preparry enemy crits, fight through shield-bros. His ability "cannot be modified" beats Mandrake/Celestian/Plague injury-aura hit-mods. Opponents overestimate him and avoid charging. **[strong consensus]**

**Strong but conditional**
- **Gunner (Plasma)** — the "delete button": 5 hits instantly kills a marine; best single shot in the game; elite-killer (great into 13+ wounds). Give **Dev1 vs marines** to fix breakpoints. Can't move + double-shoot plasma (stand still, or fire the one bolt-pistol shot). Overkill into <10W teams. Repo-dash-shoot for ~11" threat.
- **Sergeant** — not mandatory, but 2 free ploys ≈ 2 CP of rerolls, a 5th equipment slot, Lethal-5 melee & shooting. Great for beginners. **Drop him** when you'd rather field another double-shooting body (Goremongers, some Murderwing lists).
- **Headtaker (Headtaker)** — value is **P1 bolt pistol + 7" move + climb/fall cost reduction**, NOT melee. "Headtaker isn't a melee piece". Charges from conceal, guarantees kills on a 4-hit charge while taking only 1 back, plants banners on vantages, finishes injured models. Squishy (9W) into real elites/crit-stackers; bad into damage-reduction/JAS teams (Insidiants).
- **Aegis** — most-debated op; consensus has **cooled** ("actually pretty mid"). An **anti-shooting** piece, not anti-melee. Folds to Brutal / Lethal-5 / assured crits (AoD Captain, Celestians, Wreckas, Murderwing, Plague, Goremonger skullclaimer). Shines vs: shooty hordes, Stealth Suits, AoD, Aquilons, Brood Bros, Plague-caster; as durable **bait** to draw melta; holding a mid objective under smoke with Marksman overwatch. Smoke on him ignores Piercing 2.
- **Marksman** — polarizing; a **matchup-specific guard piece**, not a general shooter (can't double-shoot or shoot-then-guard at 2 APL). Hits on 2s. **Great vs:** Aquilons ("critical"), Nemesis Claw ("the bane of Nem Claw"), Sanctifiers, Stealth Suits, Vespid, Murderwing, and on Tomb World (double-guard). **Bad vs:** silent Lethal-5 snipers (AoD, Insidiants), Wolf Scouts (charge-in-conceal), Goremongers (fists only). Underrated: **double-guard** (2nd guard is free); move-dash within 2" then guard = point-blank shot ignores cover. Universal gripe: does not have a pistol.

**Niche**
- **Bombard** — ~equal to Horde-Slayer (a 1–2% swing), more niche. Best on ITD/Tomb World, into ~8–13W bodies; 5-dice torrent gets Lethal-5 (Aquilons, Sisters, Yaegirs through their DR), and forces Wreckas to burn JAS early. Low shot count hurts under JAS. Magnetize the arm — same body as Horde-Slayer.
- **Demolisher** — solid situational, not a trap. Rerolls on retaliation + relentless-on-tap carry him: 3-taps most Raveners, durable via damage reduction, has a double-shooting pistol, 1-crit+1-hit kills a Goremonger. Best into **Canoptek** (spiders/crawlers), Goremongers, Raveners, and a **Nemesis-op boss** (double-fight ~35W). Keep him out of no-reroll bubbles; never let him be charged.
- **Breacher** — near-universal **"never take" / "-1 on a 10 scale."** Horde-Slayer out-performs him everywhere. Melta bomb is heavy/short (can't move-bomb-shoot); the Servo Skull breaches walls for 1 AP anyway. Only edge: double-shooting a 4/5 profile on 3+ for double-kills on two ~8W bodies, plus a Goremonger niche. **[strong consensus]**

**Rules of thumb**
- **Only ONE Gravis per team.**
- **One-box list [strong consensus]:** Sergeant, Blademaster, Disruptor, Gunner, Horde-Slayer.
- **Generic anti-elite core:** Watch Sergeant, Blademaster, Disruptor, Gunner + a Gravis (cycle the Gravis by damage breakpoint/map).
- **Mirror-draft priority:** Disruptor and Gunner are the two must-haves — whoever gets both tends to win.
- Don't treat Gravis as backline snipers — they walk up to point-blank and anchor the line (18 wounds is hard to chew).

## TacOp Selection

| TacOp | Verdict | When to take it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dominate** | Strong | Great into other marines, elites, hordes, 10-man & Chaos-cult teams (13W = 2 tokens). Doubles the opponent's potential points, so it's a measured pick, not a wild one. Downside: losing 1 of your 5 bodies hurts more; cagey opponents can ghost/pick off the token holder. |
| **Plant Banner** | Good vs elites/semi-elites | Best on maps with safe **vantage** banner spots (Volkus, oil-pump maps where a 32mm base blocks the pump). Needs 2 marines to score+babysit → **bad into hordes and steal/last-activation-kill teams** (Stealth Suits, Murderwing, Nem Claw, Kroot, Ratlings). |
| **Sweep & Clear** | Situational | Score by clearing + denying the **centre or enemy** objective (uncontested; 2VP if also swept), plus 1VP per swept marker you control. A push-and-deny plan, *not* a home turtle: contest mid/their side and force cagey teams (Wolf Scouts/Fellgor) to interact. **Servo Skull is the enabler.** Take it when Banner/Dominate are "too dicey." Not a TacOp to *max*. |
| **Envoy** | Niche | Viable in the DW mirror (easy 4 points, limits Disruptor); won a permadeath mirror event. Counter: can't hit 4+ without giving up crit — many prefer Dominate + winning kill-op. |
| **Martyrs** | Rarely | Only on genuinely bad maps ("just die on the points"). |
| **Route** | Rarely | Occasionally on a bad map (full-send enemy side). Hard to score TP2 if you can't use TP1 counteracts to move up — don't overextend for it. |

**Banner tech [repeated & praised]:** plant just outside 5" of the edge on your last activation, then **pick up and re-place** on a later activation/counteract for ~3" of free movement into a hard-to-reach spot (a counteract isn't an activation, so you can pick up on activation and place on counteract). Servo-Thrall makes the initial Plant 0 AP. Pick-up and place are the *same* action — can't do both in one activation.

## Equipment Selection

- **Sanctus-V Bioscryer Cuffs** — the only true auto-take (also heals; removes Poison/Neutron/Terrorchem tokens *before* their effect). **[strong consensus]**
- **Scrutavore Servo-Thrall** — almost always. Opens/closes doors, breaches walls, does mission actions, moves banners for 1 AP each (~4 APL of value); enables Sweep & Clear; makes the initial Plant Banner 0 AP.
- **Smokes** (universal) — near-always: 3+ save and you shoot out unobscured (P2→P1). **Traps vs** hard-melee teams, Wreckas, and teams that ignore obscuring (AoD, DW mirror, Kasrkin) — take **Stuns** instead there.
- **Ammunition Reserve** — "always does something"; lets SIA fire twice per TP once.
- **Digital Weapons** — situational; take only when it fixes a **melee breakpoint** (Blademaster vs 7W = one-crit kill without clapback; vs a damage-reduction team). Often dropped for terrain equipment.
- **Ammo Cache** (universal) — worth it if it buys ≥1 reroll; best when the shooter stays put; pairs with obscuring + Advanced Auspex. Some prefer ladders/barricades instead.
- **Portable/mobile barricade** (universal) — underrated on a Gravis: +1 to all saves plus cover; great bait anchor with the Horde-Slayer.
- **Krak grenades** — largely unnecessary given DW's shooting; take on Aegis/Sgt to clip an 8W breakpoint
- Equipment priority when unsure: Cuffs → Servo-Thrall → Smokes → Ammo Reserve, then situational terrain/digital.

## Matchups

6-man imperial marine teams (AoD especially) are DW's hardest bracket — many firefight ploys are dead into them; "you're playing AoD but a man down." Recurring problem matchups: **Aquilons, Kasrkin, Battleclade, Nemesis Claw.**

*Ordered best matchup → worst for DW.*

| Enemy (merged aliases) | Key advice |
|---|---|
| **Stealth Suits (Xv26)** | Near auto-win (they can't trade 1-for-1). Push Aegis/Gravis to mid with Disruptor on conceal nearby (6" version, no LoS) → they must go engage and lose the shooting fight. Marksman is a house; sit back with Long Vigil. Dominate (all XV26 are 12W → 3 kills maxes it). |
| **Spectre Squad** | "Headtaker eats them alive"; charge-from-conceal is very strong. Scramble the melta/rocket. Abuse counteracts (don't trigger their fieldcraft). **Marksman is bad** (too much silent shooting). Strong matchup for DW. |
| **Canoptek** | **Demolisher's home matchup** (thunderhammer beats a crawler; losing Demo to kill one is fine). Blademaster + Gunner one-shot spiders; Disruptor punishes bad support positioning; "kill twice" near the Reanimator. Horde-Slayer/plasma needed to crack 16W optional-3+ bodies. Ignore obscuring — park just >1" behind heavy terrain for angles. Plant Banner. |
| **Hierotek Circle** | "Kill them twice." Shoot the Cryptek first. Measure the Chronomancer's teleport range (pulls models out of melee). Stay passive, dump bodies TP5 for free Kill score. Aegis is a "gigachad" here. |
| **Exodite Dragon Masters** | On Open, gun them down; on ITD/TW play smarter — Disruptor dunks on them. Dominate, focus crit-op. |
| **Goremongers** | **Do NOT melee** (near-invincible, always 2 crits). Run a melee *skew* but **walk up and double-shoot** — 5-attack marines scare the charge. Want all 5 ops able to double-shoot. | **Exaction Squad** | Aegis/Blademaster/Horde-Slayer/Sgt/Disruptor — their shields can't hurt Aegis/Blademaster; shoot the shields, charge the guns. Horde-Slayer is a "demon"; Shield that Slays makes them waste shotguns. Take the Servo Skull (their leader plants on objectives). |
| **Fellgor Ravagers** | Melee *skew* but play the shooting game — they have ~zero shooting, so abuse **Guard**. When they charge, murder them on retaliation (Blademaster's "cannot be modified" beats their injuring). Don't let your sniper get charged. Ranged crits don't kill a frenzied goat within 3" of the gong-bearer. |
| **Mandrakes** | Bait into a shootout; meleeing wastes tempo. They fear the Headtaker. Prefer Horde-Slayer over Gunner (they ignore piercing). Don't let models get isolated (they chip then finish with a fresh Mandrake). Disruptor gets around their smoke. |
| **Wrecka Krew** | Stay **10" away** (they can't move-dash-shoot). **Ignore squigs** (bait) — or shoot one next to another Ork so it blows up on its friend; disrupt a squig so it can't detonate. Body-block their breach point (not a door). Sgt + Blademaster kill in 2 crits (Wreckas can't JAS a crit). Shoot the Krusha/Demolisha. **Dominate over Banner.** Suffer Not the Alien + Lethal-5 works. |

Banner far from the tunnel; they can't Writhe while carrying a marker. Demolisher surprisingly durable (3-taps all but the Prime). Keep the Disruptor — one TP3/4 scramble swings it. | **Plague Marines** | Shoot, don't melee. Plasma brings them down; Blademaster ignores Contagion hit-mod. Headtaker removes your poison + double-shoots. Their melee is mostly Severe → Aegis weak. They can pay 1 CP to pass all resilience → only plasma is a "safe" one-shot. |
| **Wolf Scouts** | Full shooting skew (Disruptor, Horde-Slayer, Headtaker, Blademaster, Gunner — drop Sgt). Don't go melee (they outrange + punish). They ignore P1 only **once per TP** — shoot 3–4 P1 guns to force it. Force interaction with Sweep & Clear. Choose conceal (Lethal-5 chainswords). |
| **Imperial Navy Breachers** | Tanky with surprising burst; take out the heavy hitters and hide in melee from the good shooters. Don't get blasted by the Gheistskull (16" charge from behind a wall — stay 1" back). Overload Brace with damage. Aegis, Horde-Slayer, Sgt, Headtaker + Blademaster. |

| **Nemesis Claw** | **Marksman is the bane** — can't ignore obscuring, hate injury, no long-range answer. Keep the Ventrilokar outside 6". Scramble/kill the melta. Headtaker outranges in melee. |
| **Tempestus Aquilons** | **"Inverse Raveners"** — pass all 5 ops TP1–2, then all-out TP3–4. Marksman is *critical* (deters/denies the drop melta so you can play TP2). Aegis tanks a drop. Keep the Disruptor. Dominate + kill-op primary. Horde-Slayer on Volkus / Bombard on ITD. |

## Faction Rule Advice

- **Veteran Astartes** (extra action economy): in one activation you can Fight-Fight-Shoot or Fight-Shoot-Shoot (any order, two of one action) — the only restriction is on counteract, where you can't mix a Fight and a Shoot. Combined with counteracts, DW effectively get **2 activations each** (activation + counteract). Core combos: charge-hug then kill on counteract; double-shoot-and-charge an already-activated model; move-shoot or shoot-smoke on the counteract. Use it to punish opponents who charge and don't fight.
- **Special Issue Ammunition (SIA)**: applies its bonus to *every* target of a blast/torrent shoot (unlike equipment grenades, which only hit the first target). Once per TP (twice with Ammunition Reserve). Dev1 on plasma/Horde-Slayer/Disruptor; Lethal-5 on the Horde-Slayer bolter; Saturate vs any 5+ save; PC1 for indoor/condensed maps. Best user after the Horde-Slayer is the Disruptor.

## Ploys

- **Command Reroll** (universal) is the reliable pick — spend most CP here; DW's core weakness is dice inconsistency. **[strong consensus]**
- **Omni-scramble** (Disruptor's rule, functionally the team's best "ploy") — locks a model from being activated OR acting for D6 activations; stops free-action ploys, Seize-the-Initiative shots, bomb squigs. Stronger as an aggressive tool (split the enemy, shut down a table half) than defensive. **[strong consensus]**

### Strategic Ploys

- **Mission Tactics** — a TP3/4 closer that telegrapHorde-Slayer intent; also a *defensive* tool (balanced-on-retaliate → ~60% crit Aegis, more Blademaster preparry dice). But engage makes you more shootable — pick conceal vs Lethal-5 teams (Wolf Scouts).
- **The Long Vigil** — "pretty much always worth it"; triggers easily for defensive save-rerolls; lets you hang back and dare the opponent out. Needs "wholly within" — announce it out loud.
- **The Shield That Slays** — rarely worth it (also needs "wholly within"); only clearly good vs shotgun teams or into power weapons if you can't push 2 crits through.
- **And They Shall Know No Fear** — Don't reflexively spend it, though: skip it on a model that'll die outright anyway (melta) or that's barely threatened.

### Firefight Ploys

- **Advanced Auspex Scan** (Disruptor) — grants Saturate, strips the target's cover save, and lets you shoot out of your own smoke (turns off obscuring both ways). Free if the Disruptor already sees the target and isn't in enemy control range. A top ploy — smoke yourself and dare someone to look at you.
- **Auspicator Tracking** — order-swap on counteract; a standing threat that makes opponents back off / lets you flip to engage-and-shoot.
- **Suffer Not the Alien** — free vs Xenos (Wreckas, Salvagers, Canoptek, etc.); Lethal-5 on retaliation fishes for crits.
- **Transhuman Physiology** — a reactive defensive tool: fire it to survive a heavy hit (plasma) or scrape through on low HP, and to soak while trading off an operative you've already extracted value from (e.g. the Sergeant after his strat-ploy CP).

## Terrain

### Volkus / Octarius (open)

- Get your shooters onto vantage — it's where open-board Deathwatch lives — but don't camp one perch all game; keep them able to reposition to home/mid.
- Marksman on a Volkus vantage gains Accurate (2s + Precision); play him as a deterrent with repo-dash-guard to force short-range/melta teams to eat a guard shot. Skip the vantage camp vs silent shooters (they punish a static high model) — drop to ground sightlines + smoke instead.
- Bombard on high vantage into elites: sit up top, don't move, double-shoot 2 dice for a reliable marine kill.
- Headtaker delivers the banner to a Volkus top floor and threatens a 0" drop-charge from conceal (ignores falling, ~6" charge threat); teams with no vertical mobility can't shift him.
- Octarius rig-top: crowd two marines on the central pump to become near-unchargeable and near-untargetable (needs the right board side).
- Disruptor is stronger on open than close (visibility is easier to get) — elevate him on a tall stronghold, on conceal outside charge range, to omni-scramble down long lanes.
- Deploy out of cover for a faster, more efficient TP1 push — there's no infinite-range threat on open.
- Board side dictates picks on Volkus: the side with a short climb to mid favours the Headtaker, the other favours a Gravis planting banner through the stronghold wall; scout the real sightlines before committing (small-stronghold tops are often poor).
- Ladders are a Volkus staple — to reach or deny vantage and get into strongholds.
- Skip the Servo-Thrall on Volkus when playing Dominate with a crit op that needs no actions.
- Lean Dominate over Banner on open — Blast and body-stacking on points get punished; take Banner only where vantage genuinely protects it.
- Canoptek: hunt vantage shots onto their tall crawlers when they misposition (five dice + a reroll, obscured or not); ladders + smokes, and you don't need the Servo-Thrall here.
- Battleclade: they can't reach the top Volkus floor, so a lone operative can camp it for points — but the C.A.T. and Seek Light can still spot it, so it's not fully safe.
- Wolf Scouts are especially strong on Volkus — the anti-shooting board magnifies their defensive rerolls and anti-P1.

### Tomb World / Gallowdark (close quarters)

- Post the Marksman behind a hatchway on a good obscuring angle to stop the enemy moving big guns into a room.
- Horde-Slayer flamer fired into a room/stronghold or a cluster behind a door does ~9 damage via the Lethal-5 Torrent 2" profile.
- Headtaker has no climbing on Tomb World — use teleporters and hatchways for his charges instead of vantage drops.
- Disruptor drawback on Gallowdark: you measure the omni-scramble jam *around* walls, so a ~7" melta parked behind a closed door can't be pre-jammed until the door opens.
- Hatchway fight makes only ONE operative within 2" of the door a valid target (not everyone in range), and you can't hatchway-fight while already in control range.
- Smoke is ignored at 2", so it won't save a corridor operative from a point-blank melta.
- Plant Banner in a corner or tunnel-end with long sightlines and guard it easily; switch off Banner when the banner corridor has two breach points you can't defend.
- Breacher's melta bomb only earns its slot on a guard/counteract at a door.

## Generic Advice

- **HP is a resource.** Trade health for board control to enable scoring — not just bodies. Few teams can single-activation-kill a 15W 3+ model, so step out and dare them. Losing your 3rd marine without it doing anything is game-ending.
- **Guard is a signature tool** — its value is what it *stops* (an op on guard shuts off enemy melta/power pieces that can't risk shooting first). Double-guard is huge on Close-Quarters maps. Don't guard next to a door; stage marines >7.1" from any door. Guard interrupts mid-action.
- **Obscuring > cover on engage** — DW *ignore obscuring*, so park just >1" behind heavy terrain. "Everyone in a big 6-inch group hug."
- **Breakpoints matter.** Odd/prime wound counts are safer vs the common 3/4 gun (two crits through is much harder than two hits): 7W ≫ 6W, 8W is safer than it looks. Build target selection around what the enemy must roll.
- **Objectives:** commit to two lanes (usually home + mid); a 3-2 marine split is OK, **never 4-1**. Occasionally concede your home for theirs if accessible.
- **Decide aggro vs cagey at team-selection** based on rival + map, and pick ops/TacOp to match. Identify your bad board side and have a backup TacOp ready.
- **Review every game** (matchup, map, score, mistakes). The team is ~80% operative-selection + deployment; one mistake can lose it with no recourse. "Deathwatch is very much a 'know your opponent's kill team' check."
- **Decide your sacrificial lamb** for the enemy melta/P2 gun and commit — play assuming a model dies when their melta deploys.
