# Chaos Cult - Advice

## TL;DR
- **Play passive TP1–2, then explode TP3–4.** [strong consensus] Cult scales into the late game; you hunker down, out-activate, and win with three Torments on the board turn 3–4. Playing "honestly" / aggressively early loses.
- **Mutation cadence 0 / 1 / 2 / 1.** [strong consensus] Two Mutants TP1, one Torment (+ a Mutant) TP2, two Torments TP3, heal-or-one TP4. Never rush two Torments TP2 — it gimps your turn-3 spike. One Torment TP2 even if it does nothing: it dictates where the opponent can push.
- **Mindwitch is your best piece** — the only chip/ranged damage and your breakpoint-fixer. Activate her **last** to guarantee the Malefic Vortex chip; keep her concealed and screened.
- **Iconarch's damage-reduction aura is the glue** — park it within 4" of where your Torments will fight; the DR stacks with the Torment's own reduction and makes them brutally hard to shift.
- **Default gifts: Horned + Sinewed.** Fleet vs fast/elf teams, Chitinous vs shooting; Barbed and Wings only via the Abhorrent Mutation ploy.
- **Staple ploys:** Fervent Onslaught (Accurate) every go-turn; Sickening Aura on your damage turn. **Auto-take equipment:** Vile Blessing + Krak grenades + a Heavy Barricade.
- **Best TacOps: Steal Intelligence, then Track Enemy.** Everything else is situational-to-bad.
- **Math is king.** Stack Mindwitch chip + Iconarch DR + Vile Blessing to bend breakpoints; you only need to beat the opponent by one point. Cult is a high-skill, decision-heavy team countered more by bad maps than by any enemy.

## Operative Selection

- **Mindwitch — your most important operative and only real damage-at-range.** [strong consensus] Malefic Vortex chips 1–2 off models anywhere on the board (or ~10 off an elite over the game); Infernal Gaze is a strong gamble into Marines; the -1 APL strip locks a key enemy. Fix a target's wounds so a Mutant/Torment one-taps it. Activate her **last** so the chip can't be dodged. Keep her concealed and screened (Faithful Follower + a nearby Devotee) — opponents desperately want her dead. Use the forward floating head for line-of-sight/vortex lines. She only leaves the deployment zone to melt something that matters.
- **Chaos Mutant — the "protégés"; keep them alive, they are not missiles.** [strong consensus] Losing Mutants is worse than losing Blessed Blades. Stage a Mutant just outside enemy control range (move + dash to ~1.1"), then mutate next turn so the bigger Torment base reaches into engagement — this bypasses charge-reaction ploys, Stalwart, and retreat ploys. Horned "Mutant maxing" into 7-wound teams turns crit+hit into two hits and buys extra activations.
- **Chaos Torment — the payoff piece; beefy, but only inside the aura.** With the Iconarch DR up (and their own reduction) they shrug 4/5 chainswords down to 2/3. Do **not** fight Torments outside the DR field vs damage-reducing teams. A Torment can mutate to **heal** on TP4 to spike out of one-shot range. Note the current restriction: a Torment can only use weapons on its own datacard and cannot Pick Up Markers, do unique/mission actions, or throw grenades (it may still Operate Hatch).
- **Iconarch — position for the defensive aura first.** [strong consensus] Keep it within 4" of your Torments (the aura ignores line-of-sight/terrain), behind Heavy cover, concealed. The damage-reduction aura is worth far more than the +1-damage buff in almost every game. Exception: an aggressive TP2 push (via a Demagogue dash) to land the 5/5 flamer can be worth more than turtling on some maps. Don't be shy about its 4/4 torrent gun.
- **Cult Demagogue — the mastermind.** Keep her safe (~9–15" back) overseeing the board; her free Dash/Charge/Fight extends a Mutant/Torment's threat range ~3", fixes positioning, opens doors, and enables a last-activation Torment fight on a hidden target. A Torment fed the free fight can attack up to three times in a turn. She can only mutate to heal, never evolve.
- **Chaos Devotee — a precious resource *and* your speed bumps.** [strong consensus] Good vs bad Cult play is defined by Devotee management: they cap objectives, screen key pieces, evolve into Mutants/Torments, and do the mission actions/marker pickups nothing else can. Push 2–3 up as sacrificial "living barricades" to clog charge lanes while everything ramps — but don't throw them away for free, and don't over-mutate the ones you need for pickups. GA2 double-krak Devotees are effectively an extra terrain piece for TP2.
- **Blessed Blade — only good charging *together*; hold back early.** [strong consensus] Solo they whiff on 4s; paired on one target they hit on 3s and combat-support each other, reliably trading one-for-one into a Marine and getting around counterattack better than a Torment. Use them as TP2 reactive/flank pieces, bait (opponents overvalue them), objective/mission monkeys, and TP4 cleanup. Trade them freely — they're worth about a cultist. There is a genuine split on whether to hold them for a counter-punch or spend them as TP2 scoring to buy a safer turn 3; both are played by strong hands.

## TacOp Selection

Cult is locked into the Infiltration / Seek & Destroy pools, which is a real structural weakness at high level. **Do not take a TacOp as your primary objective — primary the Kill or Crit op** (Kill op is strong: you kill efficiently TP3–4).

- **Steal Intelligence — the default best.** [strong consensus] Scores 4–5 almost passively, plays *with* the Kill op, and markers persist between turns. Leave tokens out and only collect late (you can hold all five kills to TP4); you only need to hold one token TP2/TP3. A Mutant that evolves keeps its token. Great into dive/aggressive teams (they carry markers into your lines) and anything you out-body. Weakness: Mutants/Torments can't pick up, and few-model/elf teams can woodchip your carrier.
- **Track Enemy — the strong #2.** [strong consensus] Costs no AP — a concealed Accursed operative scores it just by being within 6" of an enemy that's a valid target, so it fits the cagey, out-activating plan and stages your Torments at the same time. Best into shooting/elite teams and on Volkus. Turned off by **operative-level** Seek-Light (e.g. Nemesis Claw Prey Sight) and by burrowers who leave line-of-sight; weapon-level Seek-Light does **not** shut it off.
- **Dominate — situational, weak.** Your token-holding Torments tend to die before end of turn, so it underdelivers; playable into hordes/mid-model teams where a Torment can survive on a token, and it lets you "do nothing" TP2 without pressure. Better than the picks below only in the right matchup.
- **Rout — a niche flex.** Only into low-model / hang-back teams (Stealth Suits, Phobos, the mirror) or to force a passive opponent forward on short boards. Very risky once revealed, and *you* must be in their territory.
- **Sweep & Clear — mostly weak.** The reworked version scores when the central or enemy objective is cleared and uncontested (1 VP; 2 VP if also swept), plus 1 VP for holding a swept marker — so it's a "clear-and-deny" op, and eligible objectives are the centre or the opponent's, not yours. It has some play into Raveners/Canoptek to force them onto points, but for Cult it's AP-hungry and Steal usually matches it for less work.
- **Plant Devices — the trap pick.** Requires blitzing/holding objectives Cult can't get aggressive over early without being steamrolled. Only consider it if you can *guarantee* a plant on the enemy home objective TP2 for 3+ VP.

## Equipment Selection

- **Vile Blessing (Just a Scratch) — auto-take, every game.** [strong consensus] Damage negation on a key Torment fight wins games; plan its use, don't burn it on a whim.
- **Krak grenades — near-auto.** [strong consensus] Your only ranged bite. Note only **Devotees** can throw them (Mutants/Torments cannot use grenades); the GA2 double-krak on a Marine/elf/Ogryn is as much a TP2 deterrent as a damage source — the *threat* keeps bullies honest. Best into 5+ save teams and elves.
- **Heavy Barricade — core.** [strong consensus] Torments can't hide behind Light terrain while concealed, and the Iconarch needs cover to hold the mid aura safely; mandatory on open/blast/vantage-heavy maps. Place it so the Iconarch can throw the DR aura onto the central objective.
- **Light Barricades / Razor Wire — screening tech.** Roadblock charge lanes, hide a concealed Devotee to steal a crit op, hold objectives.
- **Stun grenades — high value.** Steal points (run a Devotee in, stun, tap with another), slow aggressive teams, and neutralise Fellgor frenzy.
- **Smoke grenades — good vs shooty teams,** but wasted against anything that ignores obscuring (Phobos).
- **Mines — denial/lane-seeding**; drop one where a Torment can retreat after a kill to deny Dominate.
- **Comms Device — niche/map-dependent.** On Volkus/Tomb World it extends the Demagogue's free-action reach hugely. It works only with the Demagogue's Support action — **not** the Iconarch's Ruinous Icon (that's a Psychic action).
- **Covert Guises — situational, needs a real plan** (the Mutant slingshot, breaching a wall TP1, denying Mandrake forward spots). You roll for it and it costs a slot, so most games skip it.
- **Unholy Talisman — weak/divisive.** Only a 5+ upgrade; take it into heavy bolter fire or with Chitinous, otherwise smokes do more.
- **Baleful Script — skip.** Get your gift plan right and use the Abhorrent Mutation ploy for the situational gift instead.

## Matchups

Ordered roughly best → worst for Chaos Cult.

| Enemy | Verdict | Key advice |
|---|---|---|
| **Brood Brothers** | Favored | You can't target the Magus, so hide him in melee; slingshot a Mutant into the melta/plasma. Take Track Enemy; no Torments TP2 to even activations, then overwhelm in one turn. |
| **Kin / Kasrkin / Spectre Squad** | Favored | "Most free matchup" — Torments go through them; Sickening Aura + charge and let them shoot on 5s. |
| **Ratlings** | Favored | Ignore the 6-wound rats; Mindwitch straight for the Battlemutt (kill by end TP2), then kill the Ogryns/Bullgryns to strip board presence. Fervent Onslaught + Sickening Aura behind Heavy; GA2 kraks on Ogryns. |
| **Battleclade** | Favored | Hang back, let them push, run in TP3 with three Torments; long-range/stun grenades make it doable. |
| **Salvagers / Stealth Suits (XV26)** | Favored | Few melee threats; wait for them to advance to collect tokens. Mutate into control range in the strategy phase to beat their retreat ploy (Horned damage lands before fallback). Steal, or Rout. |
| **Void-Dancer / Blades of Khaine / Corsairs (elves)** | Good | Torments are beefy enough to bull through elf tricks. Steal almost always; Fleet to keep threat ranges symmetric; super-cagey TP1, come out TP3. Two hits on a krak kills an elf. |
| **Angels of Death / Deathwatch-lite Marines** | Good, tense | Cagey until TP3, then trade Torments 1-for-1 for Marines (you stay ahead on bodies). **Brutal** to punch through double-parry; Horned + Mindwitch chip to hit one-tap breakpoints; counter-charge. |
| **Kroot** | Even | Charge expended operatives with Mutants (they can't shoot back), evolve, double-kill; Brutal Mutants shred. Map-dependent early rush. |
| **Murderwing** | Even/Good | Kill the screecher and the matchup opens up; if forced, throw everything TP2 rather than get Routed. |
| **Plague Marines** | Even, grindy | Slow, so out-stage them; soften with Mindwitch/kraks/blades then Torments finish. Don't chew the Plaguecaster + Champion brick — kill elsewhere. Fleet + Brutal for the 9" charges. |
| **Wreckas (Orks)** | Even | Spread ≥2" for blast and the pulsa rocket; bait squigs with a Devotee; Mindwitch -1 APL + stun grenades; Horned primary, Sinewed secondary. You love the 1-for-1 trade. |
| **Exaction Squad** | Even | **Sinewed** (they ignore Sickening Aura and inflict injured penalties) + Chitinous; Brutal so they only parry on crits; Mindwitch one-taps Subductors, no healer to save them. |
| **Yaegirs (Hernkyn)** | Even, hard | Wait for TP3/three Torments; Devotees to shut off mines. Mutate into engagement (move-dash to ~1.1", then evolve) to bypass Stalwart, since you aren't charging; TP3 all-in to CP-starve them. |
| **Fellgor Goremongers** | Uphill | Need terrain to slow their huge threat range; **Sinewed**, stuns, razor wire; charge from inside a stronghold to strike first and leave them in melee so frenzy expires on their turn. Barbed's damage doesn't count as a hit for frenzy. |
| **Nemesis Claw** | Swingy | Prey Sight (operative Seek-Light) kills Track, and they fall back in the strategy phase to ruin staged charges — play honest, take **Steal**, move-block with bodies/terrain, leave Devotees as bait. |
| **Exodites / Vespids / HOTA / Mandrakes** | Hard | Expect to lose more bodies; moveblock with chained, spaced Devotees; Mindwitch the key drone/model; charge-hug fliers so they can't fall back. |
| **Deathwatch (Shield-that-Slays)** | Very hard | You likely can't kill the shield/Blademaster — kill the other four Marines and let him do his worst; deny their reach into your zone at the start of TP2 to shut off Shield-that-Slays. Sinewed + Horned Torment + Unleash the Daemon; Mindwitch shreds the Aegis; Track is the TacOp. |
| **Wolf Scouts** | Very hard | Among the worst matchups. Extra-inch move + Horned charge damage to hit breakpoints beats Sinewed (they crit-parry anyway); Acc2/Ceaseless/Brutal so they can't parry everything, and fight again if they crit-parry. The harassment dog strands itself (-1 APL) and is a free Track marker. |
| **Raveners** | Very hard | A cagey scaling mirror where one kill decides it. Crit-op lead TP2, turtle with three Torments, deny Dominate; kraks vs their 5+ saves; play away from tunnels. Steal is nearly forced (Track is rough — they burrow). |
| **Canoptek / Hierotek** | Very hard | Their castle beats yours — pressure fast before it sets up, or grind on **Track Enemy** (they can't charge you in a stronghold). Charge Guarding units (they hit on 6s); crack a spider with Horned + double-fight; concentrate kills TP3 to limit revivals; keep Mutants in two lanes. |
| **Celestians / Insidiants (anti-psyker Sisters)** | Roughest / near-unwinnable | The null field turns off the Mindwitch (no chip, no Gaze damage) and Torments hit on 5s. The Mindwitch becomes a home-objective button-pusher (Steal). Horned Torment can still 2-tap a Sister; keep the Iconarch DR up, throw kraks, avoid the "censor castle," and take the bigger TP2 risk since both teams snowball late. **Ask the TO how the aura interacts with their null field before the game.** |

## Faction Rule Advice

- **Mutation cadence 0 / 1 / 2 / 1 is the backbone.** [strong consensus] TP1 two Mutants; TP2 one Torment + one Mutant (keep a Mutant safe to present three Torment threats TP3); TP3 two Torments; TP4 heal or one more. Hard caps: max two mutations per turn, max three Torments at once (a fourth becomes a heal). One Torment TP2 "99% of the time" even if idle — it dictates where the opponent can apply pressure; two Torments TP2 is a niche anti-aggression / anti-Dominate play, not the default. **Heal is a mutation option** (a big TP4 spike). Only Devotees evolve (Devotee → Mutant → Torment); the Dark Commune can only mutate to heal.
- **Mutations are army-wide, not per-model.** A new Torment gets the army's already-chosen gifts; you can't stack the same gift twice. First gift (chosen when you make your first Mutant) applies to all Mutants and Torments; the second (at your first Torment) applies to Torments only.
- **Gift priorities — default Horned + Sinewed:**
  - **Horned** — into elites and damage-reducers; the mortals-on-charge + Brutal shreds and hits one-tap breakpoints with Mindwitch chip. Only on the charge, and the extra damage hits *other* enemy operatives, not your fight target; it lands when you finish the charge move, before enemy fallback ploys.
  - **Sinewed (Brutal)** — into melee teams; forces damage through double-parry and ignores injured Hit-stat penalties.
  - **Fleet** — primary vs 7"-move/elf teams to equalise threat ranges; otherwise save it for the ploy.
  - **Chitinous** — into shooting teams with little AP; widely found underwhelming (only a 4+), a mild new-player trap since you conceal Mutants anyway.
  - **Barbed** — no longer a default: +1 damage to one *other* enemy in your control range, once per fight sequence (not the fight target). Take it via the Abhorrent Mutation ploy for the one game/turn you're surrounded and multi-charging.
  - **Deformed Wings** — via the ploy only, for surprise charges over vantages/walls (ignores falling).
- **Core mutation tech:** the mutate-into-engagement move beats charge-reaction ploys, Stalwart, and retreat ploys. Base-blocking a Mutant so the evolved Torment's bigger base is forced to the far side of a door/wall is RAW-legal "teleport" cheese — declare intent / ask the TO, some groups ban it. You must always have a legal placement or you can't mutate (the growing base can push enemies or trigger mines). The Iconarch DR stacks with a Torment's own reduction.
- **The free Devotee → Mutant mutation on a melee kill in control range is immediate and a rare bonus — never plan around it;** a Torment attacking a turn later beats three Mutants now. Grenade kills don't trigger it.

## Ploys

- **Fervent Onslaught — near-auto on every go-turn.** [strong consensus] Grants Accurate (Accurate 2 to Mutants/Torments), so everyone lands two guaranteed hits — a huge Torment output boost. Take the accurate dice on high-wound targets; fish for crits (Ceaseless/Rending) only when you don't need the guaranteed damage.
- **Sickening Aura (-1 to hit within 2" of a Mutant/Torment) — core on your damage turn,** but the most skippable when CP is tight; drop it against accurate teams and melee ops immune to it.
- **Unleash the Daemon — your pseudo-third AP.** [strong consensus] Free fight with no requirement to have fought first: charge (or open a door), then fight; you can even charge-fight while stunned. The double-fight into two chipped Marines (add Barbed) is a key damage engine. Use it *before* the fight — no retroactive refund — and be wary vs counterattack teams.
- **Frenzied Demise — too important to skip.** [strong consensus] The death explosion is applied *before* the model is removed, so it can score Rout/Dominate and even finish the ploy target or a model you weren't fighting. Factor ~1 expected mortal into your kill math.
- **Creatures of Nightmare (-1 APL) — high-impact; "wins games."** Also forces an extra enemy body onto a point to contest it.
- **Faithful Follower ("get down Mr President") — a Devotee tanks a hit for a key piece;** always keep one near the Mindwitch/Iconarch. Note it only *treats* the follower as in control range, so it does **not** grant combat support to the original target, and a Torment protected this way does **not** project Sickening Aura's -1 from the follower's position.
- **Abhorrent Mutation — grant the situational gift (Barbed/Wings/Fleet) to the one Torment that needs it,** rather than baking it in; also to give a Devotee Wings for a last-activation objective grab.
- **Exaltation in Pain — narrow: injured operatives ignore the Hit-stat penalty.** Rarely worth it, since Sinewed usually already covers that and the team is CP-hungry.
- **CP economy is tight** (Fervent + Sickening + double-fight + blow-up runs ~6–9 CP a game). Bank CP TP1–2; don't reflexively fire Sickening/Exaltation every turn — spend on the blow-up, the -APL, and the fight-twice.

## Generic Advice

- **Play like a coward until the opponent can't win, then explode.** [strong consensus] Passive/cagey TP1–2 (sometimes sitting within 3" of your DZ), ramp Torments, hit hard TP3–4. You cannot win playing "honestly." Two valid macro styles are openly debated — the ultra-passive "castle then big TP3" line (the majority) and an aggressive "torpedo a Torment TP2 to cripple the opponent's plan" — pick by matchup and map.
- **But don't over-hide.** Push one or two Devotees up so you aren't abandoning your home objective or sitting two moves from the action on TP3; still contest mid to force cagey opponents to give it up.
- **Math and breakpoints win games.** [strong consensus] Plan every damage exchange (yours and theirs) and stack modifiers — Mindwitch lowers the target's wounds, the Iconarch reduces incoming damage, Vile Blessing keeps a Torment alive — to move breakpoints in your favour. A calculator is standard kit for this team.
- **You only need to beat the opponent by one point.** Against an over-passive opponent it's fine to do less than nothing rather than overcommit and die.
- **Out-activation is the engine.** ~14 bodies means you get the last activation — leave bait/scoring threats to force engagement, keep the real threats pending until they're on counteracts, then read the board and react. Reaching top of TP3 with 11–12 bodies means you're winning.
- **Manage threat ranges obsessively and stage just outside them.** Non-reciprocal threats are prized: a Mutant 9.1" from a covered op is uncharcheable, but on an initiative win the evolved Torment's bigger base closes to inside charge range. Use the "inside/outside" trick around thin terrain to avoid being charged around it. Keep Mutants out of enemy move-dash-shoot range.
- **The threat of a Torment often matters more than the Torment** — only commit one if it can actually kill something ("distraction carnifex" syndrome).
- **Value reliability over gambling.** The team has enough tools (Accurate, chip, DR, Vile Blessing) that you should rarely need the dice to cooperate — solve problems with flexibility and breakpoint math instead.
- **Hostage the kill grade.** Budget your losses ("let 7 die, not 8"), keep opponents at a low kill grade, and finish most games with the Mindwitch, Iconarch, and Demagogue alive.
- **Cult is countered by maps more than by teams.** [strong consensus] Octarius (2026) and Beta Decima, plus shooting-gallery ITD/Tomb World layouts, are the worst — too little cover and too-long distances for a 2-APL melee team with weak shooting and no wall-breaching or extra-APL tricks. Take first map pick at team events to dodge them, and use barricades/screening correctly.
- **Expect a steep learning curve.** This is a high-brain-load, positioning-and-sequencing team, not a run-forward horde — plan on losing several games learning staging and tempo before it clicks. Learn Steal + Track first, then positioning and hanging back.
