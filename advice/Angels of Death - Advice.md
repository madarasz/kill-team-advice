# Angels of Death - Advice

## TL;DR

- **Take the Captain.** He is the leader ~95% of games — Brutal power fist, Iron Halo (tanks a melta), free doctrine flexibility. Sergeants are niche only. **[strong consensus]**
- **"Tap the sign": Captain + Grenadier + Heavy Intercessor Gunner + 3 Assault Intercessors.** That is the default 6; everything else is matchup-driven swaps. **[strong consensus]**
- **Assault Intercessors over plain Intercessor (bolter) Warriors, always** — same-value close shooting via heavy bolt pistol, far better melee. You basically never take the bolter Warrior. **[strong consensus]**
- **You are a close-range *shooting* team that out-fights whoever charges it.** Shoot the melee teams, chainsword the shooty teams. Range past 2" rarely matters — live in the midboard and double-shoot. **[strong consensus]**
- **Flexibility is the whole identity.** Pick operatives, tactics, TacOp and equipment *after* you see map/mission/opponent. AoD rarely has a truly bad matchup because power is spread evenly and injured marines still shoot well.
- **Equipment staples: Purity Seals + Tilting Shields (vs melee) + Smoke (vs melta/shooting).** Grenadier's krak is free — never buy grenades.
- **Hardy is a strong primary but not an auto-lock** — its 5+ crit save shifts breakpoints, and that save is the whole of it, so Mobile / Resolute / Sharpshooter / Aggressive sit roughly level as secondaries; pick by matchup. **[strong consensus]**
- **Dominate is the go-to TacOp**; Plant Banner is a strong situational alternative you must defend with real bodies.
- **Trade on your terms.** Accept losing marines; aim for 2-for-1, use threat saturation and overlapping counter-charges, and charge-hug expended enemies to deny them shots.

## Operative Selection

*Order best → worst.*

- **Space Marine Captain — auto-leader, ~95% of games. [strong consensus]** Brutal power fist on 3s, plasma pistol, ~18–19 effective wounds with Iron Halo (survives a melta), and huge ploy/doctrine flexibility (free Adjust Doctrine, free firefight ploy, or Combat-Doctrine-as-firefight). Play him as executioner, as a "missile" at the enemy home objective, or as a wall that costs 3–4 actions to remove. He wins his melee with Tilting Shields / Shock Assault up. Don't burn him early — he's your guaranteed clap-back.
- **Assault Intercessor Grenadier — near auto-include. [strong consensus]** A straight upgrade on a plain Assault Intercessor: free krak grenades that *don't* count against your 2-explosive limit (the kraks carry Saturate — frequently forgotten). Krak + heavy bolt pistol "almost always kills one." Don't lead with him — keep him back so he lands kraks instead of dying first.
- **Heavy Intercessor Gunner — core take. [strong consensus he's central]** 18 wounds, 3+ save, 5" move, a bolt pistol for a second shot, and five shots that shrug off obscuring. Durable objective/banner holder and TP2 first-striker (move-dash-shoot ~10"). Tanks a melta far better than a plain marine (~30% to one-shot a 14-wound marine with Purity Seals). Best on close-quarters maps (on Guard in a hallway) and on vantage on Volkus.
- **Assault Intercessor Warrior — the staple body. [strong consensus over the bolter Warrior]** A shoot-first model (piercing-crit bolt pistol, double-shoot) with a strong 5-attack chainsword for when charged — "not the other way around." Take 3-ish; flex one into a specialist by map/matchup.
- **Eliminator Sniper — situational threat piece. [heavily debated / split].** The best sniper in the game: ignores obscuring, Seek Light, Silent (shoots on Conceal), collects safe Dominate tokens, and shuts down enemy Guard/vantage staging. Shines on open/long-sightline maps (Tomb World, Volkus 4, Bheta-Decima) and as a Battleclade / Stealth-Suit counter. **But** a skilled opponent just plays around a stationary sniper, leaving you a "dead" model and costing you threat saturation — so take him for a specific board/objective, not by default. Play him aggressively (move him up); parking him at home "has never worked."
- **Intercessor Gunner — mobility/situational pick.** Take into 3+ save / 8-wound teams (krak kills them) and use as a "second Grenadier" (repo, underbarrel krak within 2", then bolter — he can repo-shoot-shoot, unlike the Heavy). Prefer the auto/regular bolt rifle for movement; Stalker rarely worth it on him. Auto Bolt Rifle (Torrent) is a nice greedy pick into hordes (Goremongers, Brood Brothers, Chaos Cult).
- **Assault Intercessor Sergeant — niche only. [consensus: niche]** Take for the heavy bolt pistol double-shoot or a Lethal-5+ power weapon into models you must two-tap through Frenzy/high-wound breakpoints: Fellgor, Wreckas, Raveners, Ratlings, bad-save hordes. Build: power weapon + heavy bolt pistol (plasma pistol is locked to the chainsword). Note his third chapter tactic is *self-only*, not team-wide — the common mistaken reason to take him.
- **Intercessor Warrior (bolt rifle) — weak, mostly skip.** Fists are far worse than the chainsword and the rifle profiles barely beat the heavy bolt pistol; the best profile (Stalker) is Heavy/dash-only. If you must, give it Stalker and hold a firing lane.
- **Intercessor Sergeant — pretty much never.**

**List-building rules of thumb:** at most ~2 (occasionally 3) long-range shooters — a fourth shooter would be up close, where an Assault shoots just as well; keep 4 melee bodies. When trimming shooters, drop Gunner first, Eliminator second, Heavy last. You *can* field Heavy **and** Eliminator together, but rarely should.

## TacOp Selection

*Order best → worst for this team.*

- **Dominate — the current default. [consensus]** Scores an extra point vs elites and "scores itself." Pairs with the Eliminator as a safe token piggy-bank; the go-to into elites. Play cagey TP1–2, then cash safe kills on counteract in TP3. When you take Dominate, pair it with a **Kill/Crit primary** so you aren't chasing the same objective twice.
- **Plant Banner — strong situational.** Signature AoD play: plant it and force them to come to you. Treat it like a 4th objective — commit real bodies (two Astartes on a banner is very hard to shift), and place it where your team already is, not in a clever isolated spot. Plant on vantage to lock out floor-is-lava teams; use the Volkus/Octarius static-door trick (doors are ignored for control-range visibility). **Best vs ~5–8-model teams; avoid vs stun/steal teams** (Ratlings, Stealth Suits) that pluck it off you.
- **Envoy — underrated/niche.** Consistently scores 3–5 with a 4:2 melee:ranged split and aggressive assaults drawing fire; "insane" into Nemesis Claw. Avoid into Fellgor/Aquilons and telegraph-punishing teams (Raveners) — it hands the opponent agency.
- **Sweep & Clear — niche.** Scores off the **centre or enemy** objective (not your home): clear + deny it (2VP if also swept), +1VP per swept marker held. Good into Stake Claim, and to force passive teams (Stealth Suits) to come contest mid.
- **Martyrs — team-event grind pick.** Stack home + centre, concede Tac, max Crit, win on kill differential with Hardy/Resolute forcing them to dig you off points. Low ceiling and AoD are hard to kill reliably.
- **Route — doubtful.** Melee teams run gunline pieces forward and you rarely land the kills; an aggressive alt into Vespids at best.

## Equipment Selection

*Order best → worst.*

- **Purity Seals — every game. [strong consensus]** Near-universal auto-take; reaches kill breakpoints and can turn a rolled 1 into a success without rerolling.
- **Tilting Shields — almost every game vs melee. [strong consensus, top-2]** Turn off Lethal/Severe/Rending crits — effectively two free hits on a charge, and they force Hardy's math to apply. Declare *after* the opponent rolls. Clutch vs Deathwatch, Celestian Insidiants, Wolf Scouts, Mandrakes, Nemesis Claw, Corsairs, Voidscarred, Raveners. Note: does nothing vs Piercing-2 *shooting* (Battleclade).
- **Smoke — staple vs shooting/melta.** Force melta teams within 2"; combos with Auspex or Wrath of Vengeance (shoot/smoke, then shoot back out of it). **Exception: do NOT bring Smoke vs Deathwatch** — they ignore obscuring for 1CP, so it's wasted (take Auspex instead). Also weak vs Battleclade (ignore-obscuring/seek-light).
- **Auspex — situational, and rising. [growing consensus]** Take on Volkus, vs teams that bring Smokes, and to shoot cheeky angles out of your own smoke on counteract. More valuable given the Heavy commonly sits within 8" of things; the mere threat deters enemy smoke. Overlaps with Siege Specialist's Saturate — don't double up.
- **Chapter Reliquaries — situational.** Mainly vs hordes / 11+ operative teams, or as 1 extra CP specifically vs Nemesis Claw. Skippable otherwise — you're CP-rich and the Captain can Wrath of Vengeance for free.
- **Map utility — barricades, razorwire, mines, ladders, ammo cache.** Light barricades screen charge lanes and let you stand on a point in cover (must be >2" from the enemy's); razorwire is a repeatedly-cited MVP for blocking charges; mines cut a route to your banner spot; ladders get the Heavy/Gunner onto vantage; an ammo cache blocks a Ratling tripmine on the teleport pad. Placeable cover is worth most closest to the enemy — and is generally weaker into elite teams (who want *options*, not denial).
- **Extra krak/frag grenades — skip.** The Grenadier and Gunner bring their own on 3+; bought grenades hit on 4+. If you want more kraks, bring the Gunner, don't buy a slot.

## Matchups

| **Wolf Scouts** | Favored | You're beefier (5 marines + Captain) and out-shoot them; turn it into a shootout. Tilting Shields "absolutely insane" here; block obvious charge lanes, present a wall, don't feed isolated models. Mobile to no-sell the dog's strategy-phase hug. |
| **Goremongers** | Favored | Don't melee them — "gun, walk up and gun"; mass double-shooting bolt pistols wrecks them and they die on their own Frenzy turn. Chainsword is self-defense only; don't stack two Assaults in flamer range. |
| **Deathwatch** | Favored | You have 6 to their 5 and they lose faction rules/ploys vs Imperium marines. Trade 1-for-1, exploit the extra body, kill the Blademaster, respect the Disruptor/omni-scramble. **Auspex, not Smoke** (they ignore obscuring). Don't take Dominate into a 5-model team. |
| **Exodites** | Favored | Go all-shooty and keep unloading (they can't take cover). Stay mobile for diagonal shots around their intervening-cover rule; drop the Sniper, no Stalker on the Gunner, skip Siege. |
| **Voidscarred / Voidbringers (Corsairs)** | Mixed→hard | Elves are "the natural predator of AoD." Tilting Shields vs their power weapons; bait them into unsafe spots rather than charging in — they trade well into you. |
| **Voiddancer Troupe** | Favored (melee skew) | "4/5 chainswords, full send" — melee skew was the VDT nightmare. Bring one long-range shooter to keep the Death Jester honest; Resolute negates the disruptor; Tilting Shields stop them dashing out of a fight. |

| **Murderwing** | Even (skill), harder on Volkus | Don't drip-feed models — go aggressive in the open: if they shoot, you out-shoot them; if they charge, Purity Seals + Iron Halo + Tilting Shields + balanced melee invalidate their combat. Kill the shrieker/screecher first (APL warp). Dangle the Captain to eat the melta. Resolute vs their APL tricks. Auspex/ladders on Volkus; Smoke does little (they barely shoot). |
| **Mandrakes** | Even (skill) | Empty-charge, then Fight on the counteract to dodge their fight-first ploy. Take Resolute (stops stun + balefire save reduction). **Indomitus is genuinely good here** (~4+ save). Note Soulstrike bypasses Hardy's crit save (FAQ). Neutralise the big-sword model; Tilting Shields on it; take kraks. |
| **Brood Brothers** | Even→hard | Overwhelm from multiple flanks — the Magus can only brain-blast one model. Resolute vs his APL debuff. | **Xv26 Stealth Battlesuits** | Even→hard | Don't try to melee them down (they escape and gain buffs). Captain + Heavy (tanks melta) + chainswords; Eliminator keeps them honest / taxes CP. | **Raveners** | Hard (most-discussed) | Don't fight on the tunnel; shoot them (one round cripples one), bait the venomspitter, and space 40mm bases so you never offer a double-charge. **Statistically worst matchups** (cited from Pretentious Plastic Ops): Raveners, Ratlings, Hand of the Archon, Breachers, Aquilons, Fellgor, Battleclade, Brood Brothers, Corsairs. Elves broadly are the team's natural predator.

## Faction Rule Advice

- **Astartes double-act:** you may Fight and Shoot up to twice each in one activation if APL allows — but not 2 Fights + 2 Shoots. Fight-then-shoot and fight-fight-shoot are legal. To fire the main gun twice you spend the extra AP (static); use a pistol profile if you need to move. You can counteract on Conceal, and the Eliminator's Silent lets him shoot as a concealed counteraction (but you must be on Engage to go On Guard).
- **Chapter Tactics — Hardy is a strong primary, not an auto-lock. [strong consensus]** The tactic least likely to be dead (you always get shot or fought) and its 5+ crit save shifts breakpoints — but that save is all it does, so it's often run as a *secondary* and Transhuman covers similar ground for 1 CP. Near-useless vs Mandrake Soulstrike, meltas/plasma, and accurate Vespids.
- **Secondary is playstyle/matchup driven — Mobile, Resolute, Sharpshooter, Aggressive all strong:**
  - **Mobile** — becoming *the* staple. Fall back + double-shoot, fall back + shoot on counteract (with Wrath of Vengeance = pseudo-3 APL), charge *out* of a tactical hug into a backline piece, no-sell charge-hugs. Comes up every game. Limits: can't fall back *and* charge same activation; can only leave control range via Charge/Fall Back, not repo/dash.
  - **Resolute** — ignores APL/shock/stun; best as a *swap-into* (via Adaptive Tactics) rather than a locked pick — the threat of the flex does the work, and it's dead weight vs stun-free teams (Aquilons, Kasrkin). Cleanses stuns on already-expended operatives when swapped in. Key into Celestians/Murderwing/Canoptek.
  - **Sharpshooter** — guarantees a Severe crit + Accurate + Piercing-crit on bolt weapons; **procs on Guard but NOT on counteract**, and turns off after you move (repo/charge/fall back). Skews your comp toward Assault Intercessors/HBP and away from Stalker rifles; excellent on Tomb World and vs elites, hard to realise vs skilled opponents.
  - **Aggressive** — Rending on the charge (Captain two-taps most marines; a 6-0 Golden Ticket ran Hardy/Aggressive) and blocks a crit with a normal on defence. A Hardy replacement for melee metas.
  - **Siege Specialist** — "slept on": perma-Saturate denies cover saves and combat support; strong anti-Ravener and highest single-shot kill vs 8-wound models. Weak vs 3+ save teams.
  - **Stealthy** — the dud (Saturate/getting-shot-within-2" bypass it; Transhuman does its job better). **Dueller** — a defensive tool; matchup-dependent (good vs crit-reliant melee, useless vs Brutal).
- **Captain's Heroic Leader ability** grants one per turning point of: a free firefight ploy if he's the target (excl. Command Re-roll); free Adjust Doctrine; or **Combat Doctrine as a firefight ploy when a friendly AoD op activates** (still costs CP; killzone + not-in-enemy-control-range; once/TP). These are mutually exclusive. Adjust Doctrine works only during a friendly op's own *activation* (not counteract/retaliation) and still requires you to have played Combat Doctrine that TP.
- **Tournament lock:** you lock Primary + Secondary before the event and can only swap the Secondary in-game — so practise with the pair you'll lock. Most run Hardy + Mobile / Sharpshooter, or main Mobile/Sharpshooter.

## Ploys

- **Wrath of Vengeance — "the best tool AoD has," play it most turning points.** The key Mobile enabler: fall back on counteract and shoot (or dash 2" more for an Eliminator shot), or hop back and blast an unactivated enemy. Wait to see the fight/shoot result before declaring the reposition. The Captain can play it for free via his ability.
- **Combat Doctrine + free Adjust:** always run **Tactical** (Purity Seals already make melee consistent); pay for a doctrine in the strategy phase (TP3 basically always, TP2 sometimes), then use the Captain's free swap so you have Balanced whenever you act. Adjust Doctrine (firefight) ≠ Adaptive Tactics (strategy-phase secondary-tactic swap) — don't confuse them.
- **And They Shall Know No Fear — underrated.** AoD are bulky enough to keep operating on 1–2 wounds; fall-back-for-1-and-shoot combos with Mobile to keep a near-dead Intercessor working. Play it TP3/4.
- **Transhuman Physiology** — can be used to make a cover save vs a crit; a big reason Hardy isn't essential (tank the first big hit per TP for 1 CP).
- **Shock Assault** — key-kill/two-tap tool; the Captain gets it effectively free. It triggers once per Fight action (one sequence), and you only need to *retain* a crit (not land the hit) to apply the stun; can be saved for the second Fight of a multi-charge activation.
- **Indomitus — mostly bad. [near-consensus]** With a 3+ save you must fail 2+ dice for it to matter; spend CP reactively on a re-roll or ignore-injury instead. Genuinely good only vs Mandrakes (~4+ save) and low-piercing high-volume teams (Scouts, Ratlings).

## Generic Advice

- **Play aggressive; your gameplan is to run at things and kill them.** It's easy to be un-chargeable on marines when you want to be, so dictate when you're in melee. Assault Intercessors are shoot-first models with chainswords for when charged — the team was never truly melee-primary.
- **Bolt pistols mean 8" isn't as short as it looks.** Win pattern: sit in the midboard taking objectives and double-shooting within 2" to strip cover/conceal — not hanging back.
- **Trade on your terms.** Power is spread evenly, so you absorb losses better than most 6-model teams; injured marines still shoot well. Aim for 2-for-1, but a marine dying without killing anything is often fine if it opens the board. Don't smash equal profiles together — charge only with a profile edge or into a low target (Captain excepted).
- **Threat saturation + waves.** Advance in overlapping layers, each bait marine backed by a counter-charger — "make a lot of chess forks." Run two marines in charge range of the enemy's big gun so it can't safely fire.
- **Charge-hug** expended enemies (empty-charge to deny their shot, then counteract-fight or Mobile-out with Wrath) — strong vs shooty teams; **counterproductive vs melee teams** (gives combat support) and vs melee-competent gunlines (Yaegirs' plasma knives), and it slows your scoring if overdone.
- **Come with a plan and study the opponent's key rules.** You'll lose the first game into an unfamiliar team; tailor operatives/tactics/equipment to their weaknesses.
- **Line of sight is drawn to any part of the model** — banners, guns and pointing swords stick out of cover and make you shootable. Position (and magnetise) accordingly.
- **Door control on Tomb World / Gallowdark:** when ahead, keep closing doors (repo-close-dash to break LoS and hatch-fight) to lock down an area and protect Dominate/Tac leads — it wastes the opponent's APL to reopen.
- **Terrain shapes the meta:** Octarius/Volkus-light maps (flat, light-terrain vantages, long lanes) push toward shooting and are excellent for AoD — the Eliminator denies enemy vantage for free.
