# Blooded - Advice

## TL;DR
- **You are a turn 3–4 team.** Play controlled aggression, not a TP1–2 alpha strike. Poke, chip, lose only 2–3 models by end of TP2, then unload on the "go turn." This is the single most repeated idea in the community. **[strong consensus]**
- **First Blooded token goes on the Commsman, every game.** He keeps generating from there, so you have ~5 tokens and Gaze by TP3. **[strong consensus]**
- **Krak grenades are the only true auto-take equipment.** Gaze auto-crit + Reckless Aspirant's Punishing makes a krak lethal to almost anything. **[strong consensus]**
- **Backbone roster:** Enforcer + Ogryn over 2 troopers almost always; Butcher, Thug, Flenser, Sharpshooter, Corpseman, Commsman, plus plasma + melta gunners. Power-weapon Chieftain. **[strong consensus]**
- **Play for points, not kills.** Tunnel-visioning on the kill count loses games; go for 1-for-1 trades into marines and 2-for-1 into elves as a floor.
- **Steal Intelligence is the default TacOp** (~90% of games). **[strong consensus]**
- **Don't fear losing operatives** — deaths return tokens and fuel Bitter Demise. The team gets *stronger* as the game goes on.

## Operative Selection

*Order: auto-include first, situational in the middle, bench-first last.*

- **Traitor Chieftain — always, with a power weapon + auto/las pistol.** The default 90%+ loadout: auto-crit + Punishing + krak access, less risk than plasma. Chainsword + bolt pistol only into 5+ save / squishy hordes (Vet Guard, Tau, Pathfinders, Krieg, Ratlings, the mirror); plasma pistol is a niche pick into Exaction / Plague Marines / Warpcoven. A bolt-gun Chieftain has no play. **[strong consensus]**
- **Traitor Butcher — always.** Guaranteed power weapon, strong TP2 threat piece.
- **Traitor Enforcer + Traitor Ogryn — take both over 2 troopers, almost always.** They cost two selections but don't reduce your activation count and are near-always better. The Enforcer is a force-multiplier (extra fights/shoots/mission actions, forces the Corpseman to hand out relentless); the Ogryn is a "distraction carnifex" that hits on 2s with relentless, is immune to stun/shock, opens doors, and takes multiple actions to remove. Drop the **Ogryn** (never the Enforcer) into teams that reliably one-shot him — Hearthkyn Yaegirs' rockets, Hierotek, Canoptek — and on light-cover boards. **[strong consensus]**
- **Traitor Sharpshooter — auto-take.** Hits on 2s, permanent Silent (shuts down enemy Guard just by threatening), gets a free auto-crit on his hunted target, punches through obscuring, and delivers safe repeatable chip. Better into hordes than elites, but he earns a slot on almost every roster. **[strong consensus]**
- **Traitor Thug — auto-take, almost never benched.** 4/4 Brutal with damage reduction (each 3+ normal reduced by 1) makes him a wrecking ball into ≤8W teams; needs no support, great speed bump/bodyguard. Give him the FNP stim. **[strong consensus]**
- **Traitor Corpseman — near auto-include.** His relentless feeds three melee pieces (deploy him next to the Ogryn/Enforcer/Chief and drug them all TP1). Drop him for the Trench Sweeper only into a short list (Wyrmblade, Vet Guard, Pathfinders, Ratlings, Krieg) where reliability matters less.
- **Traitor Brimstone Grenadier — a top value / sacrifice piece.** Krak a single target dead, then Explosive Demise splashes D3+2 to each operative visible and within 2" (rolled separately for each). The Diabolyk Bomb is excellent (tracking darts, great for Steal Intelligence). Note the bomb is Heavy (Reposition Only) — you cannot dash-then-bomb or bomb after charging. He rarely charges (~50% to detonate on death); best used as a reposition-bomb between two enemies, or a move-dash-krak roadblock at the end of TP1.
- **Traitor Flenser — a strong "missile" / trade piece and door-holder.** Strike-on-death triggers during any fight action (yours or theirs), so he does ~6–10 damage before he's picked up; combos with Bitter Demise. Can solo a Phobos marine. Bench into Battleclade and Breachers.
- **Traitor Commsman — bring him for the token engine.** He's the home for your first Blooded token and generates more without moving; pair with the Comms Device equipment (see below).
- **Gunners: plasma always, then melta, flexing melta → grenade launcher into elves / weaker teams where melta is overkill.** Only one plasma is legal, and the Sharpshooter counts as one of your shooting slots. Ungazed gunners hit on 4s — treat a gunner shot as "a kill or a miss" and don't tilt.
- **Traitor Flenser's slot vs the Flamer:** the Flamer is a situational build — worth it into invuln / save-blocking teams (Void Dancers' Domino Field, Chaos Cult), hordes, Goremongers, elves/Mandrakes/Vespids, and on ITD — but the melta/plasma/GL trio is the default.
- **Traitor Trench Sweeper — the usual bench pick, very situational.** The shotgun no longer hits on 2s and 9W double-parry doesn't beat elf breakpoints, so you'd have to take Wicked Blades to threaten with him. Keep him only to hold a home objective against seek-light shooting (eldar birds, Fellgor shaman), in the mirror, on crit ops that pin you to a point, or where a rerollable 4+ save matters; a 3+ armour-save opponent makes him useless.
- **Traitor Trooper — situational only.** You rarely field more than 12 models. Bring troopers for specific jobs: mine-eating (Yaegirs, Ratlings), GA2 double-plant plays, or half-damage plinking — never as part of the core.

## TacOp Selection

*Order: strongest picks for Blooded first, trap picks last.*

- **Steal Intelligence — the default, ~90% of games.** Tokens persist and you need only one token to bank ~4 VP over the game; Blooded's kill volume lets you grab more late. Gets better the more enemy bodies there are and the more aggressively they dive. **[strong consensus]**
- **Dominate — good, especially into elites.** Incapacitating a Wounds 12+ enemy grants **2 Dominate tokens** instead of one, so elite targets raise your ceiling; you usually land the last hit to score the kill. Watch that your own on-death explosions don't rob you of the killing blow. Rated well above Sweep & Clear.
- **Rout (Route) — into hordes and teams you can bully** whose gunners aren't rushing your half (Phobos, Wreckas). You must be *within* 6" (not wholly within) to score. Risky now that so many teams pack Dominate.
- **Plant Devices — basically only into Canoptek / Ratlings** and crit ops that force objective-holding. Needs GA2 troopers, costs a lot to set up, and can die if you lose initiative after planting.
- **Sweep & Clear — weak; rarely a real option.** You now score only by clearing the **central or the opponent's** objective (not your own) so it's uncontested (1 VP; 2 VP if also swept), plus 1 VP for controlling a swept marker. Even so, opponents just re-contest the objective you cleared. Skip it.
- **Track Enemy — the worst; a "meme."** Near-unscorable against counteract teams (they get within 2" or out of 2" to shut it off). Don't take it.

## Equipment Selection

*Order: staple first, situational in the middle, skip last.*

- **Krak grenades — the only genuine auto-take.** Piercing, gets around Look Out Sir, forces blast movement, and combos with Gaze (auto-crit) + Reckless Aspirant (Punishing) to reliably do 8–9 damage to any operative. **[strong consensus]**
- **Comms Device — ~75% of games.** Lets the Commsman stand still TP1–2, project +1 APL, and generate a token without moving, all while still taking his other actions. Value tails off after the early game, and it's dead on some ITD maps.
- **Sinister Trophies — the best faction equipment, situationally.** Blocks Ceaseless/Relentless rerolls (not just CP/Balanced), so it's strong into accurate, reroll-heavy, melee-leaning elites — "the more accurate they are, the more effective." Interacts with Fellgor's Violent Temperament (a single 1 stops all their rerolls).
- **Wicked Blades — with troopers / Trench Sweeper, into melee teams and hordes.** Guarantees 3 damage into melee and gives gunners extra output; skip it into shooting teams.
- **Symbols of Bloody Worship — mainly for the Ogryn.** A charge-fight-krak/gaze chain can heal several wounds in one activation; also worth it into 7W hordes you beat up (Brood Brothers, Krieg). Most other ops die too fast for 1 HP to matter.
- **Ladders / barricades (light / heavy / portable) — situational but often more important than faction equips**, especially on Volkus (mobility, siege) and for parking the Ogryn or staging charges. Trench Sweeper + portable barricade is a scoring/repositioning combo.
- **Stun grenades — into Goremongers** (they hate any AP reduction) and other specific matchups.
- **Smoke grenades — rarely used;** you'd usually rather kill. Situational into Phobos (open rooms) and for out-activated teams, which Blooded isn't.
- **Chaos Sigil — a trap; don't take it.** By the time Reward Earned would matter you already have plenty of tokens, and the CP is better spent elsewhere. **[strong consensus against]**

## Faction Rule Advice
*(Dark Pacts / Blessings — Blooded tokens, Gaze of the Gods)*

- **Assign all Blooded tokens at once as the strategic gambit (free, no CP), and pick Gaze in the same gambit.** **First token → Commsman, TP1, every game** so he farms more; TP2 → the Chieftain (Gaze with just a token) plus a damage/protect piece; TP3 → whoever's left. This gets you ~5 tokens and Gaze by TP3. **[strong consensus]**
- **Run the Commsman's blood-gen TP1 and TP2 every time, TP3 situationally (a token vs. moving up to score), TP4 never.**
- **Give tokens liberally** to anyone who might do something — a dying operative returns its token to the pool first, so it's not a loss, and you *profit* a token if it dies near the Leader.
- **Gaze retains a crit** — an auto-crit with no dice value. That means Tilting Shields, Domino Field, and Nightmare Shroud do **not** turn it off; **only Obscuring stops the retention.** Gaze keeps Punishing (the crit is retained before Severe/retain-nerfs apply), which is what makes kraks so strong. A token never turns a *modified* roll into an auto-crit — an injured plasma still only gets a normal hit.
- **The Chieftain counts as gazed when he has a token OR when he's wholly in the enemy half.** A token on the Chief is therefore situational; hold him in reserve as a counterpunch or send him across the centerline. His Blooded icon returns his own token to the pool on death, so he can reabsorb one per turn — a good candidate for risky early play + Moment of Repute.
- **The Enforcer's no-injury aura is constant and lasts a full activation even if the model leaves the bubble** — enables early counteracts and wider token spread.
- **Relentless engine:** relentless on the Ogryn pre-game (Select Operatives), then the Chief and Enforcer TP1 via the Corpseman (the Enforcer can force the Corpseman to drug a third model). End TP1 with three melee ops on permanent relentless. Corpseman heals up to the wounds actually lost; each operative can only get each drug once.
- **Usually keep the Accurate 1 from a token rather than gambling on Ceaseless** (0% whiff vs ~25%); only bank Ceaseless with Lethal 5+ and combat support hitting on 2s. Accurate works in melee, on kraks, and vs obscured targets, and can stack (Accurate 2 kraks from vantage).

## Ploys

- **Glory Kill — "goated" in the elite meta. [strong consensus]** Grants relentless (e.g. make the melta relentless) to turn your 4+ gunners into real marine-killers, and it targets **any enemy operative** (no line-of-sight requirement). Reckless Aspirant + Gaze + Glory Kill is as reliable a kill as Blooded can manufacture.
- **Bitter Demise — an MVP.** On-death explosion that's much better TP3–4 when most models carry tokens (detonates on a 2 or 3, not just a 3); has put ~10 damage through in a turn. It triggers the Dominate token (you gain it, though you can't score while dead) but a Bitter Demise kill does **not** grant a kill-op VP (the damage is the ploy's, not your operative's) — Explosive Demise *does* count. Stacks with the Grenadier for a "double boom of death."
- **Moment of Repute — use it whenever possible.** +1 APL to a gazed op: charge → fight → pay CP to throw a krak. It doesn't persist to scoring/end of turn, so don't rely on it for end-of-turn objectives.
- **Reckless Aspirant** — grants Punishing (not a second auto-crit); with Gaze that's an auto-crit + auto-normal, enough to kill most ≤10W models in melee. Best on token-holders, TP2 onward, when you have initiative and a krak/charge play; it has fallen off somewhat in the elite meta where you often can't reach enemy territory.
- **Dark Favour (GDMP) — the best bodyguard ploy in the game.** Redirect incoming hits to a token-holder to make a lesser model eat the first-activation damage and to bork enemy double-kills; use the Ogryn or Flenser as the redirect roadblock. The recipient no longer needs to lack a token. Can't be used through Volkus doors (no visibility). Keep ops within 3" to set it up.
- **Callous Disregard** — shoot into combat; the discarded "fail" dice deal damage equal to their dice numbers (not normal hits), so that damage is **not** reduced by FNP or the Thug's ability, and Obscuring discards never hurt your own operative. Pairs with the Ogryn to finish a target and to shoot shields (e.g. Exaction Subductors).
- **Reward Earned — near-useless; don't take it.** You already generate kill/death/comms tokens, so you never need the extra one, and the CP is always better spent on Moment of Repute, Dark Favour, Callous Disregard, or Bitter Demise. **[strong consensus]**

## Generic Advice

- **Play for TP3–4, not early violence. [strong consensus]** If no bodies are removed by end of TP2 on either side, Blooded is winning; Bitter Demise punishes over-aggression once everyone dying carries a token. Aim to bring targets to ~10 HP (Blooded delete 10 HP easily), then have your "go turn" TP3.
- **Play for points, not kills.** Tunnel-visioning on the kill count loses games; sit on objectives and make the opponent come to you. Count the score at end of TP3 and take the guaranteed win rather than chasing kills and letting the dice decide.
- **Blooded is a setup/denial team, not a tempo team.** Stage so many spread-out threats that no single enemy activation saves the models you want dead; you out-activate most teams, so punish their moves. Build overlapping paired threats across the board (e.g. Chief + Butcher one flank, Flenser + Enforcer mid, Ogryn + Thug the other) so opponent "gotchas" matter less.
- **Don't fear death; keep threatening.** Deaths give tokens and enable Bitter Demise, and the team gets stronger late. Go 1-for-1 into marines and 2-for-1 into elves as a floor — almost every op kills efficiently.
- **The "cruise missile" line:** use the power-sword-and-krak Chief and the Grenadier as TP2 one-way missiles into enemy territory; a relentless/Punishing auto-crit power sword double-kills most ≤10W ops. Protect them with Dark Favour (keep a body within 3").
- **Ogryn deployment:** relentless pre-game, then park him in a vital spot mid/late TP2 as an unignorable threat with a chump beside him to soak close-range shots; opponents won't want to fight him, and it takes two shoot actions to remove him. On home, he stops the enemy TP1 slingshot/grapple that enables their Plant Devices.
- **Enforcer positioning:** deploy ~1" from the Corpseman, activate → direct the Corpseman to stimm → reposition; sit on an objective doing mission actions while directing shooters; commit him to fight late as a 9W bully. Enforce late vs good players. The Enforcer + Sharpshooter pairing exposes neither model.
- **Don't over-commit or bait shots early** — losing your few effective ranged weapons early usually isn't worth it, and watch for counteract return shots. Not every opportunity is a good one; losing half your team by TP2 means you're playing too aggressively.
- **Project board control with a long-range threat** (plasma / GL / Sharpshooter) on a vantage or conceal to mind-game the opponent — you have the spare bodies. But keep gunners off bad-sightline Volkus vantages; only nest them where it's a real inconvenience for the opponent (Sanctifiers, Goremongers, Raveners).
- **Melta discipline:** usually hold the melta for a Glory Kill shot; the Sharpshooter does the real chip TP2. Treat ungazed gunner shots as "a kill or a miss" and don't tilt.
