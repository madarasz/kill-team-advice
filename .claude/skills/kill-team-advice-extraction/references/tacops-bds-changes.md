# 2026 Q2 BDS (2026-05-06)

- BUFF - Dominate (Seek & Destroy tac op) — Additional Rules gained: incapacitating an enemy with Wounds 12+ now grants 2 Dominate tokens instead of 1. More tokens on the board = more VP ceiling vs elite targets.
- CHANGE (rework) - Sweep & Clear (Seek & Destroy tac op) — VP condition reworked. Old: score if you control objectives holding your Swept tokens (1VP, 2VP if also cleared). New: score if central or enemy objective is cleared and uncontested by enemy (1VP; 2VP if also swept), plus 1VP for controlling a swept marker. Shifts scoring from "hold swept markers" to "clear + deny."

# 2026 Q3 BDS (2026-08-31)

- NERF - Dominate (Seek & Destroy tac op) — VP step now only lets you cash tokens from friendly operatives in the killzone (and not incapacitated). Token-bearers off-board / in reserve can no longer score. Narrow nerf only affecting Raveners team, ommit from anywhere else.
- NERF - Sweep & Clear (Seek & Destroy tac op) — Eligible scoring objectives narrowed to the centre or opponent's objective (excluding yours).
